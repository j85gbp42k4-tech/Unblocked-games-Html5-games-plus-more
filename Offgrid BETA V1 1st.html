General

How OFFGRID starts up and what the toolbar shows.

Restore tabsWhen you come back, ask whether to reopen the tabs you had open.
Show bookmarks barShows your bookmarks in a bar under the address bar. Drag to reorder, right-click to rename, edit or delete.
Default page modeHow websites and web searches are opened. You can still switch any tab from the bar at the bottom of the page.

Browse runs the site live, like a normal tab, with everything routed through the relay. It keeps you anonymous and gets you past most blocks.

API fetches each page, or the results page of a search, in a single request and shows you the result. It gets through even the strictest blocks, but can be a little slower to load. In the expanded bar you can choose how a page is requested: Fetch (the default) or GET.

Welcome tourThree quick missions: disguise your tab, then try Browse and API mode on a real site. It shows once by itself.
